# Shipping FAQ

**How long does shipping take?** Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express takes 1-2 business days.
**How much does shipping cost?** Standard shipping is free on orders over $50, otherwise $4.99. Express is $14.99.
**Do you ship internationally?** Yes, to 40 countries. International delivery takes 7-14 business days.
**Can I change my shipping address?** Yes, until the order is packed (usually within 2 hours of ordering).
