# Refund Policy

Acme Fake Corp offers a full refund within 30 days of delivery for unused items in original packaging.
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive the item.
Digital products and gift cards are not refundable. Opened software can be exchanged but not refunded.
To request a refund, reply with your order number and the support team will create a ticket.
