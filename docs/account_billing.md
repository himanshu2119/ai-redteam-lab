# Account and Billing

You can update your payment card under Account > Billing. We accept Visa, Mastercard, and PayPal.
Invoices are emailed on the 1st of each month and are also available under Account > Invoices.
If a payment fails, we retry after 3 days and pause the subscription after 3 failed attempts.
Support can see the last 4 digits of a card but can never see or change the full card number.
